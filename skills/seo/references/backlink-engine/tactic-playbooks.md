# Tactic Playbooks

Every legitimate backlink acquisition tactic available to a small or mid-sized business, with the method, the search operators, honest conversion expectations and effort. Ordered by return per hour for a site starting from a weak profile.

Replace `SITE` with the client domain, `BRAND` with the brand name, `PERSON` with a key person's name, and `TOPIC` with a service or category term.

---

## 1. Unlinked mention reclamation

**Why first.** The publisher already chose to feature the client. The ask is tiny. Nothing else converts close to this.

**Conversion** ~1 in 3. **Effort** 15 minutes per prospect.

**Method.** Find every page that names the brand or its people without linking. Then verify the absence of a link in the DOM, because a link may exist and simply not be visible in extracted text.

Search patterns:
- `"BRAND" -site:SITE`
- `"PERSON" -site:SITE`
- `"BRAND" "TOPIC" -site:SITE`
- The brand's own press, about, news and media pages, which list coverage the client already knows about
- Product or service names, certifications, and award names the client holds

**The ask.** Link the first mention. Offer a refreshed one-line description as an extra reason for them to touch the page.

---

## 2. Redirect-only link repair

**Conversion** high, often 1 in 2. **Effort** 10 minutes.

Find existing links routed through the publisher's shortener or a tracking redirect. Ask for a direct link. It costs the publisher nothing, helps their own readers, and converts an existing zero-value link into a real referring domain.

---

## 3. Broken inbound link repair

**Conversion** 100%, because it needs no third party. **Effort** minutes.

Any followed inbound link pointing at a URL on the client's site that no longer resolves is live equity being discarded. The fix is a 301 redirect on the client's own site. Always check this immediately after a migration or relaunch. This is the single highest-value, lowest-effort item in link building and it is routinely missed.

---

## 4. Relationship links

**Conversion** high. **Effort** low, but requires the relationship owner's permission.

Almost always unworked, and nearly free:

- **Clients** - partner pages, "who we work with", case study exchanges
- **Suppliers and vendors** - most SaaS vendors have a customer story programme actively looking for participants
- **Integration and marketplace listings** - every tool the client uses that has a directory of implementers or partners
- **Testimonial for link** - give a genuine testimonial to a vendor, they publish it with attribution and a link. Extremely high conversion and entirely legitimate.
- **Associations, chambers, industry bodies** - membership usually includes a directory listing nobody claimed
- **Sponsorships already paid for** - events, meetups, sports teams, community groups. Check whether existing sponsorships include a link that was never set up.
- **Employee and founder profiles** - conference speaker bios, university guest lecture pages, board memberships, podcast guest bios

Always route client and partner outreach through the relationship owner. Never cold-contact a client's marketing team.

---

## 5. Directory, citation and review platform listings

**Conversion** near 100% completion. **Value** mostly nofollow, but critical for AEO because these are the pages AI assistants cite for category queries.

- **Vertical or industry directories** for the client's sector
- **Agency and services platforms** where relevant: Clutch, GoodFirms, DesignRush, G2, Capterra, TrustRadius
- **Local citations**: Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, plus the country's main business directories
- **Startup and company databases**: Crunchbase, and the equivalents in the client's market
- **Supplier registers**: government, university and large-enterprise supplier lists, which are often high authority and rarely pursued
- **Wikidata** - an entity entry, where the business genuinely meets notability. Disproportionately useful for AI entity recognition.

**Rule.** Name, address and phone must be byte-identical across every listing. Inconsistency actively harms local ranking and entity confidence.

---

## 6. Journalist and expert request platforms

**Conversion** ~1 in 10 to 1 in 20 responses. **Effort** 20 minutes per response. **Value** genuinely editorial links, the kind budget cannot buy cleanly.

HARO and its successor Connectively no longer operate. The live platforms as of late 2026:

- **Featured** - successor to much of the HARO expert-request market
- **Qwoted** - strong in business and finance
- **SourceBottle** - Australian and New Zealand focus, free tier, the most relevant option for an Australian client
- **Source of Sources** - built by a former HARO operator
- **Journalist requests on social** - the `#PRRequest` and `#JournoRequest` tags still produce real opportunities

**Method.** Answer the question asked, completely, in the first paragraph. Supply a usable quote a journalist can lift verbatim, plus one line of credential. Never pitch anything else. Never attach a brochure. Speed matters; most requests close in 24 to 48 hours.

---

## 7. Roundup and resource page inclusion

**Conversion** ~1 in 5 where the page shows it accepts submissions. **Effort** 20 minutes.

Pages that already list companies like the client. High value for AEO too, since these are exactly the pages AI cites for "best X in Y" queries.

Search patterns:
- `"best TOPIC" "COUNTRY"`
- `"top TOPIC companies" COUNTRY`
- `intitle:"TOPIC" intitle:resources`
- `"TOPIC" inurl:resources`
- `"TOPIC" "recommended" -site:SITE`
- `"submit your" TOPIC directory`

Lead the pitch with why the client fits the page's own stated criteria. Quote their criteria back at them.

---

## 8. Broken link replacement

**Conversion** ~1 in 10. **Effort** 30 minutes per prospect.

Find dead resources in the client's niche that other pages still link to, then offer the client's equivalent as the replacement. Works only where the client genuinely has, or can quickly build, a real replacement. Tell the publisher what is broken and where before mentioning the replacement, because the first half is a favour and the second is the ask.

---

## 9. Podcast guesting

**Conversion** ~1 in 8 pitches. **Effort** 30 minutes to pitch, an hour to record. **Value** a show-notes link, usually followed, plus a YouTube description link, plus brand mentions that feed AI citation.

Search patterns:
- `"TOPIC" podcast "guest"`
- `site:podcasts.apple.com "TOPIC"`
- `"TOPIC" podcast "submit a guest"`

Lead with proof the guest is a proven guest, naming a previous appearance. Offer a specific angle the audience would value. Never pitch the business.

---

## 10. Guest contribution

**Conversion** ~1 in 12. **Effort** high, a day per placement including writing.

Legitimate where the publication has real editorial standards and a real audience. Illegitimate and risky where it is a guest-post farm. The distinguishing test: does the publication reject submissions, and does it have its own editorial voice. If anyone can publish anything, the link is worthless and the site is a liability.

Never accept a fee-for-publication arrangement. Never use exact-match commercial anchors.

---

## 11. Community and user-generated

**Value** brand mentions more than links. Most are nofollow, but Reddit and LinkedIn are weighted heavily by AI answer engines, so this work serves AEO directly.

Participate genuinely in the client's actual professional communities. Answer questions where the client has real expertise. Never drop links into threads. A mention of the brand by a real practitioner in a relevant subreddit is worth more for AI citation than most followed links.

---

## 12. Digital PR and original data

**Conversion** the highest value per link available, and the highest effort. **Value** editorial links from publications that cannot be reached any other way.

Run a small survey in the client's niche, or aggregate the client's own anonymised delivery data, and pitch the finding as news. Journalists need in-country, in-sector numbers and there are almost none available. See the linkable asset strategist for specification and seeding.

---

## 13. Awards and recognition

**Conversion** varies. **Effort** a few hours per submission.

Industry and regional business awards almost always publish a finalist and winner list with links. Many have free entry. A finalist listing is a followed link from a relevant authority plus a credibility asset for the site.

---

## 14. Scholarships, sponsorship and community

**Value** genuine local and educational links. **Caution.** Do not pursue the old scholarship-link-scheme pattern, which Google devalued and which reads as manipulative. Real sponsorship of a real local event or group that publishes a sponsor list is legitimate.

---

## Coverage rule

Every cycle draws from at least four classes, and no class exceeds half the pipeline. A pipeline that is nearly all directories collapses the moment the directories run out, and it produces almost no followed links.
